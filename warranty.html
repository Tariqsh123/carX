What's Covered

Our comprehensive warranty protects you against unexpected costs.

Engine & Transmission

Coverage for major mechanical components including engine, gearbox, and drivetrain.

Electrical Systems

Protection for alternator, starter motor, wiring, and other electrical components.

Cooling & Heating

Coverage for radiator, water pump, heater matrix, and associated cooling components.

Steering & Suspension

Protection for steering rack, suspension arms, bushes, and related parts.

Lubrication System

Coverage for oil pump, oil cooler, and other lubrication components.

Fuel System

Protection for fuel pump, injectors, fuel lines, and related components.

Air Conditioning

Coverage for air conditioning compressor, condenser, and refrigerant system.

Plus More

Additional coverage for sensors, switches, and other electronic components.

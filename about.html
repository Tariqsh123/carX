About CarX

Your Trusted Partner in Quality Used Vehicles

Founded in 2018, CarX has grown to become one of Oldham's most trusted independent used car dealerships.

We believe that buying a used car should be a straightforward, transparent, and enjoyable experience. That's why we go above and beyond to ensure every vehicle on our forecourt meets the highest standards of quality and reliability.

From our humble beginnings, we've built a reputation for exceptional customer service, competitive pricing, and a genuine passion for matching the right vehicle with the right customer.
